The property

Scenery by day. A relaxed place to return to.

Monaara Leisure Ella brings together scenic outdoor spaces, a pool, dining areas and a range of room categories that can suit couples, friends and family groups. The view-led setting gives agents a strong visual story for guests who want to slow down in the central highlands.

Wi-Fi keeps guests connected throughout their stay, and vehicles can be arranged around each guest’s plans, from local transfers to day excursions. The karaoke room gives couples, families and groups an enjoyable option for an evening in.
