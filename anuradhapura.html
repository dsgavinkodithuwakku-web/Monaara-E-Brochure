The property

Stay near heritage, lake landscapes and local life.

Monaara Leisure Anuradhapura is a convenient base for travellers exploring the island's ancient city, Buddhist heritage and archaeological sites. The property's garden and pool setting gives guests a place to unwind between cultural outings.

Agents can position the property for cultural touring, pilgrimage visits and family stays, or pair it with a north-central itinerary that includes Sigiriya and wildlife experiences. The Monaara team can help coordinate transport, guides and excursions around each itinerary.
